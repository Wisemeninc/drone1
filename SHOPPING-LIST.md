## Order of Purchase (If Buying Gradually)

If you can't buy everything at once:

| Priority | Buy First | Why |
|----------|-----------|-----|
| 1 | Soldering kit (Eleshop.eu / Amazon.de) | Arrives in days, practice before parts arrive |
| 2 | Radio TX from RadioMaster official or n-Factory.de | Use in simulator immediately (Velocidrone, Liftoff) |
| 3 | Frame + FC + ESC + Motors + Props (AliExpress for cost, n-Factory.de for speed) | Core quad |
| 4 | Battery + Charger + LiPo bag (n-Factory.de or Amazon.de — never air-ship LiPo from CN) | Power system |
| 5 | VTX + Camera + Goggles (n-Factory.de, FlyingMachines.de, or DJI Store EU) | Video system |
| 6 | Receiver (RadioMaster official or n-Factory.de) | Control link (bind to radio) |

**Pro tip:** Buy the radio FIRST from RadioMaster official store or n-Factory.de and spend 10–20 hours in a simulator (Velocidrone or Liftoff on Steam, $10–20) before building. Simulator skill transfers directly to real flying and will save you hundreds of euros in crashed parts.
