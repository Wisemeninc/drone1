## Order of Purchase (If Buying Gradually)

If you can't buy everything at once:

| Priority | Buy First | Why |
|----------|-----------|-----|
| 1 | Soldering kit | Practice before parts arrive |
| 2 | Radio TX (RadioMaster Pocket/TX15 Max) | Use in simulator immediately (Velocidrone, Liftoff) |
| 3 | Frame + FC + ESC + Motors + Props | Core quad |
| 4 | Battery + Charger + LiPo bag | Power system |
| 5 | VTX + Camera + Goggles | Video system |
| 6 | Receiver | Control link (bind to radio) |

**Pro tip:** Buy the radio FIRST and spend 10-20 hours in a simulator (Velocidrone or Liftoff, $10-20) before building. Simulator skill transfers directly to real flying and will save you hundreds in crashed parts.
