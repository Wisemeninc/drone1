# Shopping List — AliExpress, Quad Only (3 Budget Tiers)

Just the **flying quad** components, all with direct AliExpress search links. Pick a tier and click through each row.

> **What's NOT on this page:** batteries, soldering kits, support equipment (chargers, power supplies, LiPo bags), and remotes/radios/goggles. For those, see [SHOPPING-LIST.md](./SHOPPING-LIST.md) — those items should be bought from EU stock (Amazon.de, n-Factory.de, FlyingMachines.de) because:
>
> - **Batteries** (LiPos) cannot air-ship from China — always buy from EU stock
> - **Radios & goggles** ship faster and with EU warranty from EU shops
> - **Soldering kits & chargers** are not worth the 3–4 week AliExpress wait
>
> **Shipping to Denmark:** AliExpress orders under €150 include VAT at checkout via the IOSS system — no customs surprises. Shipping typically takes 2–4 weeks.
